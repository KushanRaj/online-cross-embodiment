IDM Test Part 1: Can world-model prediction error become an RL signal?

TBD · inverse dynamics · Cosmos · LIBERO

This is a scaffold. Replace this with the actual report once the figures, videos, and claims are ready.

Motivation

RL-style adaptation usually asks for a lot of expert data or reward engineering. The question here is whether the model's own predicted future can provide an alternate signal.

Hypothesis

If a policy action implies one future, but the world model predicts or observes a different future, the inverse-dynamics disagreement may reveal task failure, correction pressure, or context misalignment.

Setup

Cosmos policy rollouts on LIBERO-10. An inverse dynamics model predicts action chunks from current and future frames. During rollout, compare the policy action against the action implied by the predicted future.

What We Measured

IDM(C, P) versus the policy action, per-step cosine distance, EEF mismatch, gripper mismatch, and success/failure splits.

Early Observations

Fill in the sharp cases: where the world model future is task-misaligned, where gripper/contact spikes dominate, and where the signal separates failures.

Open Questions

Does the signal survive better world models? Does it work under OOD futures? How much generated-future data does the IDM need? Can this become correction, not only diagnosis?
